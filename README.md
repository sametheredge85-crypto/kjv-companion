# KJV Harmony

A King James Bible you can read on your phone or computer.

**Open it here:** https://sametheredge85-crypto.github.io/kjv-companion/

GitHub Pages is already turned on for this site. The link above shows the published copy. A change appears there after it is merged into the main branch.

## What you can do

- Look up any verse, such as John 3:16, or open a whole chapter.
- Search for a word or a phrase.
- Open Harmony to read related verses beside the one you chose. The links come from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge.
- Compare two passages.
- Copy a verse with its reference, in quote marks.
- Save verses and notes on your phone. They stay on that phone.

The Bible text is stored with the app. After it has loaded once, you can read it without an internet connection.

## On an iPhone

1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

The type is large, and you can make it larger from the About page.

## The text

This is the King James Version (the Cambridge edition), which is in the public domain. Words the translators supplied are shown in [brackets]. Cross-references are from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, also in the public domain.

Nothing here is a commentary. The app shows the Bible text and the cross-references, and your own notes.
